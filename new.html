The Real Reason You Said No Last Time

Traditional veneers remove a permanent layer of healthy tooth. That's why so many women walk away.

There's a technique that keeps your tooth's natural shape and health completely intact, building over it rather than grinding into it. It's called a natural smile transformation using composite veneers.

✕ No grinding down healthy enamel
✕ No permanent removal of tooth structure
✕ No committing before you understand the process

Questions, Answered

Before you book, here's what people ask

Will any of my healthy teeth be removed?+

No. Composite veneers are built directly over your existing tooth structure. There's no grinding and no permanent removal involved.

How do I know if I'm a good candidate?+

That's exactly what the free 15-minute phone consult is for. We'll ask about your smile goals and current dental health to see if it's the right fit.

Do I need to travel to Bangalow for the first step?+

Not yet. The first conversation happens over the phone. You'll only need to visit the clinic once we've confirmed it's a good fit.

Is this the same as traditional veneers?+

No. Traditional veneers usually require grinding down a layer of healthy tooth. This natural smile transformation technique builds over your teeth instead.
